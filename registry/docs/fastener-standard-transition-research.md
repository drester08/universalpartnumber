# Fastener standard transitions and item identity

A standards cross-reference is not a same-item decision. The privately captured [Würth Connect Volume I guide](https://www.wurthindustry.uk/web/media/downloads/brochures/en_wis/connect-volume-I.pdf) illustrates why the ISO 4017 pilot must preserve exact head geometry when expanding beyond M8. Physical PDF pages 9, 10 and 32 were visually reviewed on 1 October 2026; no other pages are claimed as reviewed.

On page 9, Table 6 places DIN 933 and ISO 4017 in its exchangeable category but flags changed widths across flats for M10, M12, M14 and M22. Page 10, Table 7 separates ordinary hexagon screws from flanged screws. Its ordinary M12 columns show 19 mm for the DIN group and 18 mm for the ISO group. The guide is manufacturer-published context, not inspected normative standard text or an exact article certificate. Its interchangeability label does not establish geometric identity or universal application suitability.

## Intake decision

Retain the exact declared standard and edition when stated, the source's cross-reference relation and qualifiers, and article-specific across-flats/head dimensions. Never fill an absent article dimension from a replacement-standard label. A diameter/length match or a DIN-to-ISO successor relation cannot override a measured or declared geometry conflict. Do not broaden the current M8 comparisons into a global DIN 933 equals ISO 4017 alias.

Proposed future capture should distinguish `declared_conformance`, `supplier_cross_reference` and `successor_standard` assertions, with evidence and scope for each. These are design proposals, not new approved mappings or SQL schema fields. Obtain exact variant drawings and governed edition/option review before expanding candidate coverage.
