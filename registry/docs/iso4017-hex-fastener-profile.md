# ISO 4017 fully threaded hex-head fastener pilot

Status: draft and unverified for UPN issuance. No equivalence decision or UPN has been created.

## Scope

This pilot covers individual metric steel hex-head screws explicitly identified to ISO 4017 and fully threaded. It excludes partial-thread bolts, inch threads, nuts, washers, studs, thread-forming screws, prevailing-torque features, special patches, and commercial kits. Pack quantities and GTINs are recorded separately so a pack identifier is not mistaken for the identity of one physical screw.

## Exact articles under comparison

- Bossard article `1049860`, EAN `7611914041916`, BN 56, M8 × 30, ISO 4017 / DIN 933, steel 8.8, fully threaded, zinc plated blue, 13 mm across flats, 5.3 mm head height.
- Würth article `00578 30`, EAN `4011231104012`, M8 × 30, ISO 4017, steel 8.8, full thread, zinc plated and blue passivated A2K, 13 mm across flats, 5.3 mm head height, product class A, declared corrosion-protection period 48 h.

## Why no UPN is issued

The sources align on the principal standard, diameter, length, thread extent, head and drive form, geometry, material, strength class, and coarse surface family. They do not yet prove the same thread pitch, coating specification, dimensional product class, or corrosion-performance context. Bossard's generic “zinc plated blue” statement must not be treated as proof of Würth's specific A2K finish.

The automated screen therefore returns `insufficient_evidence`. A reviewer should obtain an exact Bossard technical declaration for article 1049860 before considering equivalence.
