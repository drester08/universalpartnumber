# ISO 4017 fully threaded hex-head fastener pilot

Status: draft and unverified for UPN issuance. No equivalence decision or UPN has been created.

## Scope

This pilot covers individual metric steel hex-head screws explicitly identified to ISO 4017 and fully threaded. It excludes partial-thread bolts, inch threads, nuts, washers, studs, thread-forming screws, prevailing-torque features, special patches, and commercial kits. Thread direction is required. Pack quantities and GTINs are recorded as supplier offers so a box identifier is not mistaken for the identity of one physical screw.

## Exact articles under comparison

- Bossard article `1049860`, EAN `7611914041916`, BN 56, M8 × 30, ISO 4017 / DIN 933, steel 8.8, fully threaded, zinc plated blue, 13 mm across flats, 5.3 mm head height.
- Würth article `00578 30`, EAN `4011231104012`, M8 × 30, ISO 4017, steel 8.8, full thread, zinc plated and blue passivated A2K, 13 mm across flats, 5.3 mm head height, product class A, declared corrosion-protection period 48 h.
- Fabory-branded article `01210.080.030`, EAN `8715492030054`, M8 × 30, ISO 4017 / DIN 933, steel 8.8, fully threaded, electrolytically zinc plated, right-hand 1.25 mm pitch, 13 mm across flats, 5.3 mm head height. Its official generated PDF states full-box ordering in quantities of 200.
- Böllhoff article `40178.8VZD830`, M8 × 30, ISO 4017, steel 8.8, fully threaded, 1.25 mm pitch, product grade A, 13 mm across flats, 5.3 mm head height, and a 200-piece pack. Its VZD finish is zinc plated and thick-film passivated, Cr(VI)-free, with a minimum 5 µm coating and stated ISO 9227 performance of 72 h to white rust and 144 h to red rust.

Fabory also prints NSN `5305-12-337-0503`. It is retained as a source-asserted external identifier and research lead, not as proof that any other article has the same NATO codification. An authoritative codification record must be obtained before the NSN can support an equivalence decision.

## Why no UPN is issued

The sources align on the principal standard, diameter, length, thread extent, head and drive form, geometry, material, strength class, and a coarse electrolytic-zinc surface family. They do not yet prove the same thread direction, thread pitch, coating specification, dimensional product class, or corrosion-performance context across every record. Bossard's generic “zinc plated blue” statement must not be treated as proof of Würth's specific A2K finish; Fabory's generic electrolytic-zinc statement does not prove either blue passivation or Böllhoff's VZD coating. Böllhoff's thick-film VZD specification is explicitly different from the blue finishes and therefore creates a coating conflict, not a match.

Four of the six pairwise automated screens return `insufficient_evidence`. The two Böllhoff-to-blue-finish pairs return `hard_conflict` on coating specification. A reviewer should obtain exact technical declarations for the fields still unstated before considering any remaining equivalence.
