# KLINGER UK Maxiprofile specification research - 1 October 2026

The official [UK specification](https://www.klinger.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/maxiprofile.pdf) is now privately captured. All three complete pages were rendered and visually reviewed. The printed issue is 01, dated 28 August 2019; the 2025 upload folder is not the issue date. It is family context, not an exact supplied article drawing or certificate.

Page 2 contains four facing and 21 core material options. Fourteen explicitly bounded research-label comparisons with the South African document yield seven differing published maximum temperatures: 316L, 347, 321, Monel 400, Nickel 200, Inconel 600 and Inconel 625. Both raw values are retained. These correspondences are not approved material mappings, and no value becomes an article rating or universal material default. Similar/grouped labels such as B-2/B-3 and grade-specific titanium are deliberately not coerced into the South African labels.

Page 3 explicitly identifies LA3's floating guide ring and LA2's lack of a guide ring. LA1 is described with a guide ring, but fixed attachment is not explicitly stated. CA variants use corresponding convex styles. These distinctions cannot establish the style of a supplied row whose article construction is unknown.

The page-2 drawing labels core and facing and local serration geometry, not the historical three-value diameter tuples. That evidence gap remains open. Page-1 refurbishment and certification claims are publisher context only; neither reuse permission nor independent certificate verification is granted.

`scripts/check_klinger_uk_maxiprofile.py` binds the original PDF and immutable South African research report by SHA-256, reproduces the 25 rows and 14 comparisons, and preserves scope exclusions. Six tests cover counts, dates, disagreements, unmerged grade labels, attachment scope and changed evidence. `reports/klinger-uk-maxiprofile.json` is staged research: no registered source count, article observation, SQL profile, CSV correction, approved mapping, equivalence or UPN is added.
