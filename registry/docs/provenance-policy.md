# Provenance and evidence policy

## Trust tiers

- **Tier 1:** standards bodies, official classification publishers, regulators, and manufacturer-controlled technical documents.
- **Tier 2:** manufacturer-controlled product pages and authenticated data feeds.
- **Tier 3:** authorized distributors and established technical aggregators.
- **Tier 4:** marketplaces, resellers of unknown authority, forums, search snippets, and other discovery-only material.

Identity decisions require Tier 1 or Tier 2 evidence for each manufacturer part. Lower-tier sources may populate a research queue but not a canonical specification.

## Required evidence fields

Every accepted observation must identify the publisher, source title, canonical URL, retrieval date, exact page/section/table locator, observed part number, and review state. Retained files additionally require a SHA-256 checksum.

## Licensing

Public access does not mean open reuse. Each source is classified as open, attribution, restricted, review required, or unknown. Restricted sources may guide manual verification when access permits, but their protected content must not be copied into the registry without authorization.

## Corrections

UPN keeps the original observation, records the rejection or supersession, and adds the corrected observation. Public-facing corrections should name the affected record, reason, evidence, reviewer, and effective date.
