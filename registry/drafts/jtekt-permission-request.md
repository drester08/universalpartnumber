# Draft JTEKT data permission and shield clarification request

Unsent draft for the project owner to review. No recipient, sender identity, permission or commercial agreement is established. Do not send without the user's authorization.

Subject: Permission to reference JTEKT bearing data in the Universal Part Number registry

Dear JTEKT bearings team,

We are developing Universal Part Number, a source-backed parts registry that separates manufacturer articles from supplier listings. Our aim is to help users identify a part accurately without suggesting that matching dimensions alone make two bearings interchangeable. No JTEKT equivalence has been approved.

We would like written permission to retain bounded evidence privately and publish selected factual bearing attributes, manufacturer designations and source references in a searchable registry and API. We are not requesting permission to republish full catalogues, page layouts, product images or logos.

For an initial pilot, could you confirm the permitted scope for your `6205 ZZ` and `6204 ZZ` product pages, the bearing-number guide, BS004 section B, and the Japanese and English deep-groove family pages? Please identify the covered URLs or catalogue revisions, allowed fields and uses, including structured extraction, normalization, public display, API access, comparison and source links. Please also specify attribution wording, restrictions, validity dates, revision handling and any revocation process. If an approved data feed or licensing contact is more suitable, please direct us there.

Separately, could a technical representative confirm the shield material and non-contact construction applicable to these exact standard ZZ articles, with a current source or drawing reference? The code guide and catalogue provide sidedness and contact-form context, but we do not infer missing article properties from suffix absence. We also need article-specific evidence for bore construction, cage, clearance, tolerance, bearing material, supplied lubricant and locating features before completing identity records.

Please distinguish technical clarification from permission to reuse and publish the information. If you can authorize the proposed use, please identify the granting organization, the authorized representative and the precise scope in writing.

Thank you.
